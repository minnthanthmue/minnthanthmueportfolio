# Profile Photo Instructions

Place your portrait photo here:
1. File name: `profile.jpg` (and optional `profile.webp`)
2. Location: `/public/images/profile.jpg`
3. Recommended resolution: 256x256px or 512x512px square crop
4. The site uses `<picture>` to serve `profile.webp` and `profile.jpg` with `object-fit: cover` and `object-position: center 20%`.
5. Until you replace `profile.jpg`, the site displays an accessible fallback neutral gray circle with your initial "M".
